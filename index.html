Mix & Match Bot Modules

Alongside the ready-to-use bots, the package also includes interchangeable bot modules.

You can easily customize any included bot to your liking: add, swap or remove different personality sections, scenario definitions and example dialogues.

For example, you might use the Standard bot but replace its scenario with the Extended version, or add the Combat Behavior module from the Extended personality to one of the Story bots.

Mixing modules may create a few duplicated prompts, but these are easy to spot and remove.
