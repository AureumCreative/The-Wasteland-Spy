About the Package

If you're a Deacon enjoyer and already like my current Deacon bots , you're in for a treat!

This package expands on my public Deacon bots with lots of new and improved content that can also be used with my Fallout Lorebooks.

I personally had an absolute blast with these bots. Between all the different scenarios, I ended up exchanging over 4,000 messages with my private versions, before even releasing this package. So... I'd say I can recommend them. 😄

• • •

So what has been improved? What is new?

  • An even more authentic portrayal of Deacon, with completely rewritten personality characterization. Faithfully captured his layered humor, compulsive lying, conversational style, and emotional depth.
  • Three Core versions with different levels of detail, allowing you to balance immersion and token usage.
  • A wide range of new scenarios exploring different genres, relationships and roleplay dynamics — from open-ended sandbox adventures to emotionally driven or story-rich narratives.
